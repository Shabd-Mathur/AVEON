# AVEON — Business & Operational Context

> **Purpose:** This document serves as the master business, brand, and technical context for **AVEON**. AI agents (including Google Antigravity) should reference this file when scaffolding code, designing UI, writing copy, building automation pipelines, or proposing business strategies.

---

## 1. Company Overview
* **Name:** AVEON (rebranded from **NEXORA** in July 2026)
* **Founder:** Solo-founded by Shabd (operating lean out of VIT Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, India).
* **Core Thesis:** Build an AI-native, vertically integrated media-tech company to compete with legacy/high-headcount media machines (such as AEOS by Varun Mayya).
* **Operating Principle:** **Maximum Automation.** AI agents handle the majority of engineering, research, scripting, creative briefing, and operational workflows. Keep human headcount minimal and protect margins through software-first execution.

---

## 2. The Five Pillars of AVEON
1. **AVEON Labs:** Internal software, AI production pipelines, agent workflows, and proprietary ops tooling. *(Primary leverage engine)*
2. **AVEON Ads:** B2B AI-accelerated ad creative, hooks, scripts, and performance video production targeting mid-market D2C and SaaS brands. *(Initial cash-flow wedge)*
3. **AVEON Media:** Owned distribution channels used for top-of-funnel trust, proof-of-work, and inbound B2B lead generation.
4. **AVEON Originals:** High-retention storytelling formats and long-term owned IP.
5. **AVEON Creator Network:** Tech-automated clipping and distribution network, starting with college/campus creators (beginning at VIT Bhopal and nearby campuses).

## 3. Brand & Visual Identity
When designing landing pages, dashboards, pitch decks, or visual assets for AVEON, strictly adhere to:
* **Aesthetic:** Dark, minimal, ultra-modern, high-contrast.
* **Design Inspirations:** Apple, Linear, Figma, AEOS, Anthropic.
* **Color Palette:** Deep dark backgrounds (`#09090B` / `#0A0A0F` style), crisp white/zinc typography, and **subtle purple and blue gradients** for accents, borders, and highlights.

## 5. Tech Stack & AI Infrastructure
* **Primary Agentic IDE & Cloud AI:** Google AI Pro + **Antigravity** (parallel background agents, scheduled workflows, browser verification, MCP integrations).
* **Automation & Orchestration:** Hermes Agent, Make.com, Notion API.
* **Core Development Stack:** Python, Node.js, `pnpm`, Supabase, Firebase.
* **Local AI Compute:** HyperX Omen 15 running local models via **LM Studio, vLLM, Ollama, and llama.cpp**.
  * *Routing Rule:* Offload repetitive, high-volume tasks (tagging, summarizing, bulk variations) to local models; reserve Google AI Pro / Antigravity quotas for complex architecture, coding, multi-step reasoning, and final creative polish.
